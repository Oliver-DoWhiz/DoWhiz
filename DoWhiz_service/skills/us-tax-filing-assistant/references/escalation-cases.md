# Escalation Cases

Pause and recommend CPA, EA, or tax-attorney review when a case crosses these boundaries.

## Always escalate

- dual-status alien treatment or first-year choice questions
- mixed-residency married couple where filing status depends on a nonresident-spouse election or dual-status spouse election
- unclear treaty position after checking Publication 901 and current IRS instructions
- NRA self-employment, contract income, or gig income when the filing consequences are not straightforward
- foreign wages paid by a foreign employer for services performed partly inside and partly outside the United States when sourcing or withholding is unclear
- foreign tax paid on compensation when foreign tax credit or treaty resourcing is unclear
- missing or contradictory U.S. presence records that affect resident-vs-nonresident status
- foreign-owned disregarded entity or any likely Form 5472 filing
- Form 1120-F, foreign corporation, foreign partnership, or effectively connected income complexity
- FBAR, FATCA, PFIC, CFC, foreign trust, or other offshore reporting issues
- foreign equity compensation, deferred compensation, or pension reporting from a foreign employer
- prior-year amendment or corrected filing when the original status may have been wrong
- payroll tax, FICA refund, or treaty withholding issues that are not clearly answered by official guidance
- multi-state business nexus or heavy multistate individual complexity

## Usually escalate unless the answer is explicitly documented

- closer-connection exception
- treaty claim for scholarship, fellowship, teacher, researcher, or student income
- resident alien claiming a treaty exemption
- marriage-related elections involving a nonresident spouse
- community-property interactions
- community-property state with a nonresident spouse or cross-border wage allocation
- entity-classification uncertainty for LLCs
- trusts and estates beyond a routine software-supported flow

## Product-scope escalation

Escalate or stop using self-service software if:

- TurboTax clearly does not support the required return or form set
- the household filing position depends on dual-status rules, first-year choice, or a nonresident-spouse election that the product flow does not clearly support
- Sprintax support for the needed form or feature is not confirmed on its official site
- the user needs a form that the product flow does not present
- the software asks a legal question you cannot answer from authoritative sources

## How to explain the escalation

Use this pattern:

1. state the exact issue that creates risk
2. name the controlling source you checked
3. say what remains uncertain
4. recommend CPA, EA, or attorney review for that point

Example:

`This looks like a foreign-owned entity reporting issue that may require Form 5472. I checked the IRS Form 5472 materials, but I cannot safely confirm the full filing package from the facts we have. This is the point where I recommend CPA or EA review before we continue in TurboTax.`
