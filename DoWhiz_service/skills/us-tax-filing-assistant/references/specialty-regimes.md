## Exempt organizations and nonprofits

Use this section when the user mentions a charity, nonprofit, church, private foundation, tax-exempt entity, Form 990, Form 1023, Form 1024, Form 990-T, UBIT, revocation, or reinstatement.

### Intake facts

Collect:

- legal entity name and EIN
- exempt-status subsection if known, such as 501(c)(3), 501(c)(4), or private foundation
- whether the organization already has an IRS determination letter
- tax year or fiscal year end
- approximate gross receipts and total assets
- whether the issue is annual filing, exemption application, unrelated business income, revocation, reinstatement, or notice response
- state of formation and any states where charitable registration or activity matters

### Documents to request

- IRS determination letter, if any
- prior Form 990, 990-EZ, 990-N, 990-PF, or 990-T filings, if available
- organizing documents, bylaws, or exemption application materials when recognition or reinstatement is the issue
- current-year revenue and expense statement and balance sheet
- notice letters from the IRS or state regulators if the problem is a compliance failure

### Intake prompts

Ask only what is missing:

- Is the organization already recognized by the IRS as tax exempt, or is it applying now?
- Which filing is the issue: Form 990, 990-EZ, 990-N, 990-PF, 990-T, 1023, 1024, or something else?
- Did the organization have any unrelated business income or business activity?
- Is the problem a late or missing filing, a revoked status, or a new exemption application?

## Retirement plans and employee benefit plans

Use this section when the user mentions a 401(k), 403(b), SEP, SIMPLE, solo 401(k), defined benefit plan, Form 5500, Form 5500-EZ, plan correction, EPCRS, or employee benefit plan administration.

### Intake facts

Collect:

- plan sponsor legal name and EIN
- plan type
- plan year
- whether the plan is a one-participant plan, an ERISA plan, or another arrangement
- approximate participant count
- whether the issue is plan startup, annual filing, late filing, correction, termination, participant notice, distribution, or rollover
- third-party administrator, payroll provider, recordkeeper, or custodian involved

### Documents to request

- plan document or adoption agreement
- prior Form 5500, 5500-SF, 5500-EZ, 5558, or 8955-SSA if relevant
- participant census or participant count summary
- trust or account statements when balances or contributions matter
- correction notices, compliance statements, or IRS or DOL correspondence if the issue is a failure or audit

### Intake prompts

Ask only what is missing:

- Is this a participant tax question on an individual return, or a plan-sponsor administration question?
- Which plan type is it, and how many participants are covered?
- Is the issue a Form 5500 or 5500-EZ filing, a late filing, a correction, or a plan document problem?
- Has the sponsor received any IRS or DOL notice, or is this a voluntary correction?

## Digital assets

Use this section when the user mentions cryptocurrency, bitcoin, ethereum, stablecoins, NFTs, tokens, wallets, staking, mining, airdrops, DeFi, or Form 1099-DA.

### Intake facts

Collect:

- whether the owner is an individual, business, trust, or exempt organization
- exchanges, brokers, wallets, or custodians used
- activity types: buy, sell, swap, payment received, payment made, staking, mining, airdrop, NFT transaction, or other disposition
- whether the user has transaction exports and basis records
- whether any Form 1099-DA, 1099-B, 1099-MISC, or 1099-NEC was received
- whether the activity looks like investing, compensation, business income, or inventory
- whether foreign exchanges or offshore accounts are involved

### Documents to request

- exchange CSV exports or annual transaction reports
- wallet transaction exports if noncustodial wallets were used
- Form 1099-DA, 1099-B, 1099-MISC, or 1099-NEC if received
- basis records, acquisition history, and prior-year capital-loss carryforwards if relevant
- records of staking, mining, rewards, or business receipts if ordinary income may be involved

### Intake prompts

Ask only what is missing:

- Did you only buy and hold digital assets, or did you sell, swap, receive, stake, mine, or otherwise dispose of them?
- Did you receive any Form 1099-DA or other broker reporting form?
- Are the records complete enough to compute basis, proceeds, and income, or are transaction exports still missing?
- Is the activity personal investing, compensation, self-employment, or a business operation?

## Routing and escalation cues

- Do not assume Sprintax or TurboTax is the right route for exempt organizations, retirement plans, or digital-asset-heavy cases.
- First identify whether the work is a normal income-tax return, a separate information return, or a specialized compliance or correction matter.
- Escalate early if the facts suggest nonprofit revocation or reinstatement, private-foundation excise taxes, retirement-plan correction or disqualification risk, prohibited transactions, incomplete digital-asset basis, or offshore exchange complexity.
